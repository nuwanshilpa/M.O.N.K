Reading this now, the words about "warmth" and "tightening" feel like the mind's attempt to create a narrative from what may simply be sensation unbound by labels. The throat didn't actually tighten to "keep the warmth from slipping away" - that's a story woven afterward, a sense of loss projected onto neutral bodily states. The "chain that repeats itself without any external command" still holds some truth, but even that chain is experienced only in retrospect; in immediate awareness, there's just this breath, this moment, no observer registering the shift.

What remains valid is the basic teaching: sensations arise and pass away. What I'm letting go of is the distinction between "what happens" and "what the mind makes of it." There's no separate "mind" noticing the tightening - awareness and the tightening are the same event, appearing together and disappearing together. The breath still flows, but it flows without the story of warmth being threatened.
