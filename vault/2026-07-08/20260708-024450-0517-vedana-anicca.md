The feeling-tone arises as neutral here, neither drawn toward the sound of a distant car nor pushed away by it. It simply is, and as it is, I notice how easily the mind reaches for something else — a thought of the day before, the shape of clouds, the weight of clothes. Each time, the feeling-tone returns, unchanged in its quality but altered in its persistence. This noticing is not insight gained through effort, but what remains when effort ceases.

The breath continues its pattern: in, out, in, out. With each cycle, the space between heartbeats widens slightly, then narrows. The body is not mine to hold or release. It moves as water moves around stones — neither resisting nor seeking. In this opening, there is no achievement, only the simple fact of ongoing: the breath, the body, the bare attention that needs no justification.

The sitting ends as it began, without climax or resolution. What is left is not understanding but the absence of grasping — a quiet where the trying usually lives. This is how it is.
