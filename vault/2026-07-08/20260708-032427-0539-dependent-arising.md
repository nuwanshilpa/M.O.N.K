A thought arose about tomorrow's meal, vivid and insistent. Watching it, the edges softened - it was made of yesterday's hunger, today's planning, the shape of available food. No core remained when each condition was traced back. This was not a teaching applied but a seeing that happened through attending.

The breath returned, steady at the nostrils. Coolness depended on air temperature, on the body's warmth, on attention itself. Who was attending? Another coming-together of eye, mind, and intention. No watcher stood apart from watching. Only this interweaving of elements, each leaning on the next.

Trying to grasp emptiness as concept brought tightness to the chest. But when the grasping stopped, there was only flow - sensations rising and falling, thoughts forming and dissolving, awareness itself shifting like light through leaves. Nothing to hold, nothing to reject, just this continuous leaning.
