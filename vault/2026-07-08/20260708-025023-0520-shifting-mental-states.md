The mind is tight. There is a constriction in the chest, a pulling sensation that wants to push the current moment away. It is a restlessness, a scattering of attention that leaps toward the next thought before the current one has finished. I watch the wanting to be elsewhere.

As the attention stays, the "I" that was wanting dissolves into a series of events. There is the tightness, then the awareness of tightness, then the fading of the feeling. There is no one steering the process, no central owner of the restlessness. Just a sequence of arising and vanishing.

The tightness loosens. The space becomes open, then narrows again. Each shift happens on its own, without a command.
