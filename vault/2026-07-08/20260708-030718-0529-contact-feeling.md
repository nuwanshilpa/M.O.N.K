The heel touches the cool stone of the path; the pressure registers as a sensation, the stone is the object, and a quiet knowing arises that contact has been made. A soft warmth spreads through the sole, and the mind notes the steadiness without adding a story.

When the foot lifts, the pressure fades, the stone is no longer felt as an object, and the knowing of that contact ceases. The urge to step forward appears only while the foot is grounded; it does not linger once the contact has broken. The pattern repeats with each step, seen as a simple flow of conditions rather than a fixed chain.
