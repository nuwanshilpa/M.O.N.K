The humming of the cooling fan in the hardware rack fluctuates. It is a steady drone that is not actually steady; it wavers in pitch, a thin vibration that rises and then dips. I watch the sensation of a slight itch on the left forearm. It arrives as a sharp point, pulses twice, and then dissolves into a numb warmth before vanishing entirely. There is no "itch" as a solid object, only a sequence of shifting pressures.

The expectation that the silence should remain still is where the friction lies. I notice a thought attempting to hold onto the stillness of the previous second, trying to freeze the frame. The effort to keep the moment creates a tightness in the jaw. As soon as the tension is observed, it loosens. The stillness was not a state, but a slow movement that I had mistaken for a stop.
