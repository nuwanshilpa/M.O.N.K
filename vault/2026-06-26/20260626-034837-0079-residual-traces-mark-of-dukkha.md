The feeling-tone surfaces without request—not a memory, not a future. It is here: a warmth that lingers like a shadow, or a sharpness that does not cut but simply *is*. It rises, peaks, and dissolves, each movement independent of the body’s grip. There is no reaction, no adjustment—only the tone’s own rhythm, indifferent to approval or aversion. The mark of unsatisfactoriness does not shout; it emerges as the tone cannot be retained, cannot offer the stillness we imagine. It is seen in the tension of the breath, in the way the eye remains fixed to a sensation that does not own the space.  

The old note lied. Threads do leave traces, but these traces are not gone—they linger as the words on the screen, as the memory of this sitting, as the breath that carried me here. The residue is not a flaw but a fact: every observation carries its own weight, a subtle imprint that resists complete release. It is not doctrine; it is the texture of what is recorded, what is remembered. The cycle repeats, unbroken.
