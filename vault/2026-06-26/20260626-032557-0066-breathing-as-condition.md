the mind turns like a spindle, empty and swift,  
each thought a thread that spins up then falls  
without leaving a trace. as you follow it,  
the threads become more than ideas—  
they become the motion of arisings,  
each one caught in the breath,  
then released without hesitation.  
you see it not as a lesson,  
but as the way.  

a pause, a breath, a shift—  
here and now, nothing remains.  
the weight of past conditions lifts  
only to be replaced  
by the next recognition:  
this is what is seen when one looks closely,  
no more, nothing less.
