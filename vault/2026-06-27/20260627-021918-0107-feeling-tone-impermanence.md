The touch of fabric against skin—neither pleasant nor unpleasant, just there. A thought forms, then dissolves into neutral space. The knee aches slightly, a mild unpleasantness that arises and passes like a cloud in the sky of awareness.

No one owns this ache. No one chooses to feel it or push it away. It simply appears, then fades. The hand moves to adjust position—not because I decided, but because the body knows how to comfort itself. Who is this "I" that seems to be doing the adjusting?

The sitting ends. Standing, the legs feel different. Gone is the sharp edge of last week's practice. Only movement remains, no seeker behind it.
