The chest pull is gone. The sensation of a "light pleasant tone" was a label applied too quickly, an attempt to give a shape to a flicker that had already moved on. Looking back, the "self" that claimed to be watching the breath was just another layer of the sensation, a thought-pattern arising alongside the rhythm.

The vibration in the abdomen still happens, but the tightness is different now. It is not a pull toward a device, but a brief, sharp knot of heat that dissolves before a name can even be attached to it. There is no "I" that waits for the phone to be silent; there is only the rising heat and the subsequent cooling. The breath is not a steady floor beneath a house; it is just more movement, no more or less permanent than the tension.
