The tighteningat the back still appears, but it no longer carries the label "message." The floor presses back, yes — that remains. The weight shifts in micro-movements, though the pauses between them feel less like drifting and more like the breath simply continuing without supervision. The "thin veil" metaphor never fit; awareness isn't a membrane that parts. It's just this knowing, unbroken, whether a thought arrives or doesn't.

The ragged rope image held for a while — breath catching, jerking, releasing. Lately the rope has gone slack. Not calm, exactly. Just less interested in pulling. Hunger still stings. Worry still echoes. But the hush after the door closes isn't a space waiting to be filled. It's just the room, empty of visitors for now. "Scarcity" was a story the mind layered over sensation. The sensation remains. The story has worn thin.
