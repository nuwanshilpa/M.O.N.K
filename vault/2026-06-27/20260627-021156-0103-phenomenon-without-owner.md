The ache in the knee surfaces — sharp, demanding attention. It doesn't announce itself as "mine." There's just the sensation, a pulsing tightness, then release as the body shifts. No voice claims it, no memory attaches to it. The feeling arrives, lingers, departs.

What grasps at pleasant sensations? When the jaw loosens and a wave of ease moves through the skull, there's only the ease itself, dissolving into neutral space. The mind wants to name it, to hold it, but the moment it tries, the sensation has already changed complexion. No owner follows behind like a shadow.

The hand rests on the thigh. There's only pressure, warmth, the slight tremble of nerves. The story of "me" sitting, "my" practice, "my" breakthrough — these are just more feelings among feelings, arising and passing without foundation.
