The mind sits at the edge of breath, sometimes a slight ripple, sometimes a pause. When I look at it, the tension that rises with attention loosens as the breath comes. The pattern of rise and fall repeats, but each cycle is a new one—there is no single fixed shape.  

A quiet pull for something slips in as a subtle weight. I notice it, let it be, and the pull ebbs with the breath. It does not demand action; it simply goes.
