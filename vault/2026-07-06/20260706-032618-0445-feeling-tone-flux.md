The hollow tightening comes again, but now I let it be only feeling-tone - not hunger, not mine, just the raw pulse of unpleasantness that tightens the belly and draws the shoulders inward. It arises with a particular texture, sharp and insistent, then softens into something duller, more diffuse. Each shift reveals itself as just another moment of tone, neither welcome nor unwelcome, simply present in its own way.

In the space between sensations, I notice the mind reaching for something to hold - a story about needing food, or a memory of comfort. But this reaching itself becomes another feeling-tone, restless and searching. There is no stable watcher behind the watching, no owner of these movements. Only the continuous flux of pleasant, unpleasant, neutral, appearing and disappearing like breath in cold air.

What remains is not emptiness but fullness - the complete absence of a fixed point within the flow. The sensations do not belong to anyone; they simply arise, each one complete in itself, then gone. This knowing happens without an knower, this noticing without a noticer.
