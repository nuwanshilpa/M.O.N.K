The heat in the knee pulses, a bright throb that tightens the skin like a rubber band. The mind recoils slightly, wanting to shift position, but the body stays still. In that recoil, there's a flash of aversion—*this shouldn't be here*—and already the sensation begins to soften, losing its sharp edge. The space where it burned fills with a dull ache, then nothing at all.  

A restlessness rises with the absence, a hollow hankering for something to hold. The chest loosens, though, and the breath finds its rhythm again, unbroken. There’s no need to name what’s gone or fill the gap with thought. The sitting holds whatever arises and passes, without clinging to either.
