The breath moves in and out, and somewhere in the space between inhale and exhale, there's a subtle shift - the sense of "I am breathing" dissolves into just breathing happening. Watching this closely, the body feels like a current rather than a container, sensations arising and passing without a fixed owner receiving them. The urge to claim this experience as mine still flickers, but when I follow it to its source, there's only more mental chatter about claiming.

Thoughts appear like clouds - shape and drift - and each one carries that familiar tag of "my thought" until I look directly at the tagging itself. It's just another mental event, this claiming, no different from the thoughts it claims. The hand touches cool mat, and instead of "my hand" there's only pressure and temperature meeting skin, both sensations flowing through the same awareness without divider.

When pain comes - a sharp signal from the knee - the immediate reflex is to push it away, to make it not-mine. But watching this pushing, I see it too as just another passing formation. The pain, the resistance, the relief when it fades - all empty of any central commander. What remains is just this knowing, this bare witnessing without ownership.
