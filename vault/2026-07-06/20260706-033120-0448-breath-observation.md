The tension in the throat did not really loosen so completely as remembered - it lingered beneath the breath's surface, a subtle tightness that returned between exhales. The mind's floating parallel to sensation was not so clean; there was a quiet pull toward ownership of that expansion, a whisper of "this is my calm" beneath the letting go.

The craving that arose was not just about physical comfort but carried a sharper edge - a wanting for the observing itself to be different, better, more complete. The breath remained tied to the body's small grievances, and the stillness before tension and craving was not as separate as described, but mixed through with the same unease.

What stands: the breath marked the body's presence, the noting happened, something shifted. What fades: the certainty of distance between watcher and watched, the clean release, the pure observer untouched by what it observes.
