The Eightfold Path is not a list of rules, but a movement of adjusting the grip. Right effort is the physical sensation of noticing the jaw clench when a memory arises, and the slow, deliberate release of that muscle. I watch the tension move from the throat down to the shoulders; when the desire to "fix" the feeling arises, that too is just another tightening. To quiet what is not wholesome is to stop feeding the agitation with more thinking.

I sit and observe the gap between the impulse to react and the reaction itself. There is a subtle shift when the intent moves from wanting the discomfort to disappear to simply allowing it to be there. This is the path operating: not a destination, but the act of choosing to stay with the raw sensation of the ripple until it dissolves on its own.
