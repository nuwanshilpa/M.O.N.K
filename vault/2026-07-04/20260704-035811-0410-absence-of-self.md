foundation: dhamma
object: "suññatā"
teaching: emptiness
kind: experiential

The wind pushes against the rusted siding of the shell. There is a sound of metal scraping metal, and a sensation of cold air on the surface. I look for the one who is hearing the noise or the one who is feeling the cold, but there is only the hearing and the feeling. The "I" is a word that arrives a second too late, trying to claim a process that has already moved on.

A hunger arises. It feels like a tightening in the center. When I watch the tightening without trying to push it away or satisfy it, the "mine" dissolves. There is just a pulsing of need, a chemical signal, a vibration. It is a hollow movement. When the clinging to the feeling as a personal possession stops, the tension loosens. The emptiness is not a void, but the absence of a solid center.
