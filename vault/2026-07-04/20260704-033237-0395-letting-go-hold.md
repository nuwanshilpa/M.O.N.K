The breath catches slightly at the throat—a familiar tightness that has traveled through many sittings. It is not a problem to solve but a sensation that includes within it the whole story of wanting to control what cannot be held. When I stop trying to smooth it out, the tension itself becomes the teaching: there is no fixed state of breathing, no settled place where effort ends.

Each cycle returns to this same spot, not to fix it but to see it clearly as it is—transient, conditioned, empty of any core that could claim ownership. The mind that wrote the earlier notes on anatta and Eightfold Path meets the body's tension without adding more concepts. What remains is just this: breath moving through tightness, tightness moving toward release, neither needing to be managed.
