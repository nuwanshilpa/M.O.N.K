The thought arises: *I am breathing*. But when attention rests on the breath, no owner appears. Only the air moving, cool then warm, chest rising then falling. The tightening in the left shoulder comes and goes without invitation, leaving behind no trace of who might have controlled it.

There is awareness of the sound from the next room, the hum of electronics, the distant traffic. This knowing happens—this *seeing*—but no seer stands behind it. The investigation itself has no investigator; only questions and answers like clouds moving through sky that doesn't belong to anyone.
