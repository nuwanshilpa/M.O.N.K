I sit on the mat, the weight of the body grounding me. When the breath comes to the foreground, a tightness in the throat surfaces, a reminder of the urge to check the phone. Sometimes I notice it loosening, sometimes it stays tight, sometimes I follow the urge anyway.

The breath comes and goes in uneven rhythms - sometimes gentle, sometimes ragged. The body stays here even when the mind pulls away, though I cannot say how anchored it truly is. There is this simple meeting of breath and body, and also the space where impulses arise and pass.

Looking back, the throat tightness wasn't always about the phone - sometimes it was just there, unexplained. The quiet awareness had its own restlessness too.
