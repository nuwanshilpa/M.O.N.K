The mind sits like a still pond, yet small ripples appear when a thought of needing more food surfaces. The thought swells faintly in the chest, the surface of the pond brightening for a moment, then the ripple slows, the calm returns, and the surface is again flat. It comes, it fades, like a breath—neither clung to nor rejected, just seen as it is.
