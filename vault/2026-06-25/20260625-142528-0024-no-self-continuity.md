The thought of needing food appears - who decided this was *mine* to need? The sensation arises in the chest, bright and clear, then dissolves back into ordinary awareness. No owner, no controller, just the movement itself.

I watch the mind's surface, noticing how 'I' doesn't precede the thought, doesn't claim it as personal. The ripple happens, then the knowing of the ripple happens, then the return to stillness - but who is it that returns? Only the continuing, without a thread to hold.

The hunger thought was never mine to begin with. It arose from conditions, disappeared into conditions, leaving nothing behind that could be called 'me' or 'my experience'.
