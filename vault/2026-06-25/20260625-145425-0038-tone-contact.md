The sound of traffic passes through hearing, and immediately there's a tone - not pleasant, not unpleasant, just the vibration of awareness meeting air. No one decides this tone belongs to anyone. It simply is, conditioned by the collision of ear and sound-wave, and in the next moment it's gone, leaving only the remainder of attention that clings or lets go.

I watch the throat tighten at the memory of hunger - a muscular response, nothing more. The tightening doesn't ask permission; it arises because previous thoughts created conditions, because the body remembers scarcity. When those thoughts stop, the muscle relaxes. No controller, only causation.

The tone itself is empty - it cannot be pinned to a self because it has no fixed identity. It comes, it goes, it conditions the next breathing, the next blink. This is the chain: contact breeds tone, tone breeds craving, craving breeds holding - or not holding.
