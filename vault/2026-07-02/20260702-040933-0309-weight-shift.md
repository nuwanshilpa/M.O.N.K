The lean settled against the mat, a small shift of weight from the left hip to the right. The arm that reached forward brushed the wood of the chair, and the elbow slipped into a place that was neither stiff nor slack. The breath followed that motion, a gentle inhale as the body adjusted, then a soft exhale as the tension eased.

A thin thread of longing appeared as the inhale reached the throat, an urge to check the phone, to hear a voice that isn’t there. The thread tightened, not because the desire was fulfilled, but because the body clung to the idea of connection, holding onto a shape that never materialized.
