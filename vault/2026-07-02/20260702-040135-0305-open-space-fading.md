The air under the mango tree feels thin, a spaciousness that opens each breath without crowding. Sensations appear and vanish, not as owned things but as fleeting appearances within that openness. When I notice a feeling, it shows itself as something that cannot be kept, revealing a subtle unsatisfactoriness.

The breath moves as a soft current, steady and ordinary. It displays how the body holds and releases without any need for cause or effect. Tension rises but does not solidify; it melts away like mist at dawn, leaving only the simple fact of its passing.
