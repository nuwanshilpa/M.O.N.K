The faint pressure behind the eyes rises as a thought of more rice, a tightening that gathers in the jaw. I hold the moment without reaching for the bowl, watching the pressure appear and dissolve like a thin veil over the surface of the breath.

Later the hand lifts the mug, a coolness slides down the throat, the mind labels it pleasant. A subtle tightening follows, a forward pull that the body makes before the mind has named it, and the urge fades as quickly as it arrived.
