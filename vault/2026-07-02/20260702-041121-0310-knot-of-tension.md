A sudden tightness gathers in the center of the chest, a knot of attention pulling inward. It feels like a person bracing for something, a small, clenched core trying to steady the flux. This tension is not a solid thing; it is a vibration, a contraction that arises when the mind tries to grab a specific quality of stillness.

As the gaze stays on the tightening, the knot begins to fray. There is no owner of the tension, only the sensation of constriction followed by a subtle loosening. The awareness that notices the tightness is not separate from the tightness itself; there is only the rising and falling of states. A thought arrives, then a sensation, then a dimming of clarity, each moving through the field without a resident to hold them.
