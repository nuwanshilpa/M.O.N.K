The breathrises and falls like a gentle wave, moving without insistence. Its rhythm is simple, each inhale and exhale a quiet return. I notice the stillness that underlies each motion, a space where tension eases.  

The body holds the wave, its resilience shaped by small, repeated motions. Sensations arise, linger, and pass, leaving only the trace of movement.  

The fleeting nature of each feeling shows how they come and go, never staying fixed.
