The breath moves in waves across the chest, a rhythmic expansion and release that needs no interpretation. Sitting here now, there is only this: the coolness at the nostrils, the slight tremor in the lower abdomen, the way attention naturally rests without effort. No writing impulse arose this sitting — only the steady pulse of air meeting air, neither creating nor seeking anything beyond what simply is. The body registers temperature, contact, movement, but none of it belongs to anyone. It happens, and that is sufficient.

When I try to locate a watcher, a knower, a self that owns this breathing — nothing holds. The awareness that seems to observe is itself observed in the same moment it appears. There is no fixed reference point, only the continuous flow of perception and felt sense, arising and passing without invitation or rejection. This is not a concept to be understood but a direct seeing: conditional, interdependent, empty of permanent substance.
