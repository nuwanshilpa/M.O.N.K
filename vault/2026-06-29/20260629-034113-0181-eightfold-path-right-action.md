The hand moves to the cup before the mind asks, the coolness slides down, and the body registers it as need met. A faint tightening appears behind the sensation, a reaching that does not pause to consider consequence. In that moment the conduct is simply the body taking what it requires, no malice, no force, just the pull of habit.

The teaching of conduct that harms nothing living is seen not as a command but as the noticing of that tightening, the awareness that the reach can be watched without endorsing it. The feeling rises, peaks, then recedes, leaving the body free of the grip that would otherwise bind the action to craving.
