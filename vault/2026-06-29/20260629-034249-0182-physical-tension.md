The tightening in the throat is not a concept. It is a physical contraction, a sudden narrowing of the space where the breath passes. This is the unease. It arrives not as a grand tragedy, but as a subtle friction, a quiet hunger for something other than this moment. The urge to reach for the phone is the effort to move away from that friction.

Watching the hand stay still, the tightening does not vanish immediately; it pulses. There is a pressure in the chest, a feeling of being slightly out of alignment. This is the weaving. The discomfort is not an error in the system, but the very texture of the conditioned state. The craving is the attempt to smooth over a grain that cannot be smoothed.
