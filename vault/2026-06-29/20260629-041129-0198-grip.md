The mind is currently gathered, but it is a brittle kind of gathering. There is a pulsing in the center of the forehead and a slight lean toward the left. A thought arises: *I am focusing well.* Immediately, the gathering loosens. The "I" is just another ripple, a brief tightening of the mental grip that arrives and then dissolves, leaving only the open space where the thought was.

There is no one managing the attention. There is only the seeing of the focus, the seeing of the distraction, and the seeing of the gap between them. The sensation of "owning" the awareness is seen as a secondary overlay, a thin film that appears and disappears. When the film is gone, there is only the process of knowing, occurring without a center.
