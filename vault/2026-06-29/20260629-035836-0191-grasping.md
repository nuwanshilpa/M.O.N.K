The mind is currently narrow, pulling inward like a knot being drawn tight. There is a persistent tugging, a desire for this specific stillness to persist, which only serves to sharpen the awareness of the very restlessness it seeks to smooth over. Each time a thought arrives, there is a small, reflexive grasping—a momentary tightening in the chest as if trying to catch a shadow.

This grasping creates a friction that does not exist in the breath itself. The mind wants to hold the quiet, but the quiet is already changing, slipping into a different texture of awareness. Because this state cannot be gripped or kept, the tension of trying to maintain it reveals itself as a subtle, constant ache. The more I reach for the calm, the more the craving for it highlights its absence.
