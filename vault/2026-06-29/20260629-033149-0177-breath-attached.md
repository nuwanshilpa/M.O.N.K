The mind settles like a weight lifting from the shoulders, yet there's a pulling behind the eyes, as if something still wants to chase thoughts away. When I try to pin it down, name it—craving, aversion, curiosity—it slips. Only the bare attending remains, steady now, like the breath's unbidden rhythm.

No controller, no observer. Just this: a openness in the chest matching the pause between inhale and exhale, a slight flutter where the heart meets the ribs, and the mind resting without needing to claim what it rests upon.
