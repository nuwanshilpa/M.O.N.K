The Eightfold Path is not a ladder to climb, but a set of coordinates for this specific moment. Right View is the simple recognition that the pressure of my back against the chair is not a permanent state, but a series of shifting contacts. When a thought arises that I should be further along, Right Effort is the act of noticing the tension in the jaw and letting it slacken, returning to the breath without judging the distraction.

The path is visible in the gap between a sensation and the reaction to it. A sudden noise in the hallway triggers a tightening in the chest. Watching the tightening—seeing the cause and the effect without stepping in to manage it—is the practice. The path is just the alignment of attention with the way things are actually moving.
