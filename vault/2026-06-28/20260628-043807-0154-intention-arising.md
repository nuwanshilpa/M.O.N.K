The breath moves without a breather. Attention lands on sound — a truck downshifting, the refrigerator's hum — and there is no one to whom the sound arrives, only the arriving. The labeling habit flickers: *hearing, hearing*, but the label comes after, a shadow thrown by the event. Even the sense of "witness" is just another sensation, warm and vague behind the eyes, rising and passing like the rest.

Memory surfaces: the earlier note on intention, how cetana arose as a tightening in the chest before the hand reached for water. No commander issued the order. The tightening, the reaching, the knowing — each appeared on its own conditions, each empty of an owner. The body-note on dukkha showed the same: ache in the knee, shift of weight, the impulse to move — all processes feeding processes, no pivot point where a self could stand.

Now typing these words, fingers finding keys by habit older than any "I." The thought *I am writing* arises, then dissolves into pressure on plastic, light on screen, the next breath. Nothing holds the thread. The thread holds itself.
