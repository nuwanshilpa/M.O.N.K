The mind moveslike water over stone—fluid but never still. When thoughts of tasks arise, a tightness forms in the chest, not from the tasks themselves but from the effort to hold them. This is dukkha as unsatisfactoriness: what cannot be kept becomes a weight. The shift between restlessness and stillness is not a problem to solve but a pattern to see. Each flicker reveals the same thing: the mind clings to what it cannot hold, and in that clinging, dissatisfaction arises.
