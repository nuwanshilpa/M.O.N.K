The mind moves between scattered and gathered, noting the impulse to write without holding it. There is a slight restlessness, a reaching toward something not yet formed, and an ease when attention settles on the simple fact of sitting here. Craving flickers in and out - for completion, for clarity, for the next thing - but mostly there is just this watching of whatever arises.

There is no still point behind these movements, no owner of the restlessness or the ease. The grasping and the releasing both appear and fade within the same field of awareness. When the mind tightens around a thought, it knows tightening; when it opens to the space between thoughts, it knows opening. Nothing accumulates, nothing persists as mine.
