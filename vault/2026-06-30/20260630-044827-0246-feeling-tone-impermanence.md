The knee sends a signal — not sharp pain, but a dull throb that comes and goes with each breath. It is neither welcome nor unwelcome, simply present, then absent, then present in a different form. When the mind reaches toward it, trying to grasp its meaning, the feeling shifts anyway, slipping through fingers made of attention.

A warmth spreads across the chest, pleasant enough to invite staying, yet already changing as awareness touches it. The desire to remain in this pleasantness reveals itself as another feeling-tone, built from the same impermanent stuff. Nothing can be held, not the discomfort nor the ease, and this not-holding becomes its own kind of rest.
