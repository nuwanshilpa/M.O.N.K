The cool air against skin shifts from neutral to pleasant as breath settles. A sound drifts through the room — pleasant, then fading. Neither draws the attention; they come and go while the body sits. There is no need to hold what is already leaving.

An itch arises, sharp and unpleasant, pulling at the knee. It builds, peaks, then dissolves into neutrality. The relief feels pleasant for a moment, then too passes. Each tone knows its own ending. What stays constant is the movement away from each arrival toward each departure.

This cannot be kept. The pleasant fades. The unpleasant lingers only long enough to remind. Neutral reveals itself as temporary too. In sitting with this flow, the restlessness of reaching shows itself — not as teaching but as exhaustion from chasing what will not remain.
