Sitting here, the feeling of fabric against skin appears - a coolness through the robe, warm air at nostrils. Each sensation carries its tone: the pressure of spine on cushion holds a neutral weight, while tightness in the jaw hums with unpleasant friction. Thoughts drift through these same channels, some pleasant in their ease, others sharp with irritation.

No hand adjusts the robe, no mind chooses the breath's pace. The pleasant loosens into neutral, the unpleasant softens without fading. Who moves the shoulder blade or releases the clenched stomach? The doing simply does itself, leaving behind only what was done - and even that slips away.
