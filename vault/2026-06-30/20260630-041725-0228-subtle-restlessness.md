The old note speaks of a tightening chest, a pressure to produce. Reading it now, I feel a slight dryness in the throat and the weight of the device in my hand. The "I" who felt that flutter is gone, yet the pattern of wanting to be seen remains. It is a low-frequency hum, a subtle friction that arises whenever the mind attempts to capture a moment rather than simply being in it.

The unease is not a concept; it is this specific itch of restlessness. It is the gap between how this moment is and how the mind wishes it to be. I watch the tension in the jaw flare and fade. There is no "truth" to believe in, only this tightening and this releasing, repeating without a center.
