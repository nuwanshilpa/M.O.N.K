The breath entered and exited without volition, each movement etching the contours of the body like a scribe’s hand on parchment. A knot ahead of the left shoulder tightened with the in-breath, unraveling only halfway through the out-breath, as if the impurity had no escape route. The spine, though meant to rise, leaned imperceptibly to the right, revealing the weight of habit as gravity’s patient teacher.  

Below, the soles of the feet pressed into the mat, their arch flattening under the body’s pull. A second of slackness there, and the posture would cave; the breath would waste itself on compensating tremors. The hands, palms down on the knees, shifted once—thumb out, ring finger in—like a flicker of distraction, then settled.  

The element of earth held the structure, air the breath, water the fluid tension, fire the heat beneath the knees. No self-shaped vessel owned these textures. The impermanence of the breath’s contour was certain, yet clung to as a map. The body, in its doing, showed itself as a hollow experiment.
