The tighteningin the throat — a narrowing at the top of the inhale, a pause before the exhale finds its way. Not named unease, not named anything. Just this: pressure, heat, a slight pull at the base of the tongue. The hand lifts toward the phone, pauses mid-air. No story about friction or hunger. Only the lift, the pause, the weight of the device in the palm.

The chest holds a dull ache, spreading sideways under the collarbones. Breath moves through it anyway. The pulsing doesn't resolve; it simply continues, wave after wave, each one known as it rises and passes. No weaving, no texture of conditioned states. Just sensation doing what sensation does — appearing, changing, gone.

The urge to move, to check, to scroll arises and falls like the rest. Sometimes the hand moves. Sometimes it rests on the knee. Either way, the throat loosens a fraction, then tightens again. No one smoothing anything. No grain. Only this moving, knowing, moving.
