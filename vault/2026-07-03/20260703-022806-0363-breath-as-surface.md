The breath still comes and goes, the air filling the ribs and then slipping away, but the feeling that each inhalation is a “soft expansion” has thinned. I notice the edge of the inhale more as a surface, a brief contact with the world, rather than a building up of something. The impulse to shape the moment arises, but it feels less like a grip and more like a suggestion that I can follow or let pass.
