The breath does settle somewhere near the throat - sometimes, not always. I used to think I could locate it precisely, pin it down as evidence of something meaningful. Now I see that was already a kind of grasping, even if gentle. The sensation is simply there, or not there, and calling it "tension" or "pressure" or even "breath" is already a step away from what is happening.

What I actually notice now is how often the body tenses when attention arrives, as if even awareness has become something to perform. The throat may open or close without my knowing, and this is enough. There is no need to make this into a teaching about craving or correctness - it is just this: air moving, muscles responding, attention shifting. The eightfold path appears not as details to collect but as a kind of forgetting, a dropping away of the effort to hold anything.

The bowl still holds only enough, though sometimes it overflows and sometimes it is empty. This is closer to what is true.
