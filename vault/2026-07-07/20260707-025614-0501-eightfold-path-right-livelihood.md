The breath pauses, not because the air has run out, but because a subtle tug appears in the throat—a soft fist of wanting that tightens the chest. I watch the sensation as it rises, settles, and lets go, noticing it is not a self that feels tightness, but a fleeting event that appears and vanishes on its own. The feeling itself is just the meeting of breath and desire, a pattern that can be seen without needing to name it or hold onto it.
