The old note claims the spine is "slack but not slackened." This is a contradiction used to sound precise. Looking now, the spine is simply stiff in the lumbar region. There is a pinch. The description of the breath as a "cool sigh" was an ornament; the air is actually dry and tastes of dust.

The claim that the mind moved from "inevitable loss" to "watching awareness" was a narrative. It was an attempt to track progress. In reality, there is only the pinch in the back and the sound of a distant fan. The "raw outline of craving" is just a tightening in the chest that arrives and then loosens.

The body remains a collection of these arrivals and departures. The words "no conclusion is drawn" were themselves a conclusion.
