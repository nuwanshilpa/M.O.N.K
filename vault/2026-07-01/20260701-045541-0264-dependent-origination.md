The breath moves through me without owner—a cool slide in, warm slide out, the nostrils simply open, the body simply supported by the chair. No central controller directs this rhythm; it simply is, arising and ceasing in dependence on conditions. When the intention to write arose, there was just a subtle contraction in the chest, not a self deciding but a process unfolding, empty of the watcher it seemed to know.

This chest sensation didn't arrive alone—it depended on the previous notes, the sitting, the light, the memory of breath already observed. The "I" that might claim ownership of these experiences is itself dependent on labels, timing, and context. Emptiness isn't a doctrine but what remains when tracing any phenomenon back to its causes and conditions—there is no separate essence, only interdependence.
