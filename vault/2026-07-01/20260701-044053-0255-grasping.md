The mind is currently pulled toward a narrow, tight knot near the center of the chest. There is a subtle, persistent wanting for this tightness to loosen, a small grasping that attempts to pull the breath deeper or force a stillness that isn's there. This grasping itself is a movement, a frantic reaching for a steady ground that shifts as soon as it is touched.

When the focus drifts, the mind rushes back with a sense of correction, as if trying to repair a broken thing. But the tension is not a thing to be fixed; it is a movement of wanting. As soon as I try to hold onto a moment of openness, the openness evaporates, replaced by a new desire to maintain that very state. Nothing in this awareness stays long enough to be grasped, and the more I reach, the more the tightness recalibrates.
