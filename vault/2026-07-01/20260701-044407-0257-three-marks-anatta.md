The citta that rose in the earlier note was not a thing that could be held; it appeared, was felt, and then let go. In that passing there was no owner, no center that could claim the experience as its own. The same opening showed up in the later note about vedana: the feeling that arises is simply a ripple, not a possession, and it carries the same lack of a self‑owner.
