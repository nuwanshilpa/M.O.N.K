Here I sit, breath unfolded like water over stone. The body is a river—shifting, always moving, never fixed. I notice the breath’s quiet rhythm, the subtle places of warmth and coolness, the soft tingle of skin against air. There’s no need to grasp; just observe what arrives, as it comes and passes.  

This moment is a whisper: the world of craving dissolving in the stillness. I see the mark of unsatisfactory change, not as a lesson but as a truth made visible. The cycle of feeling is here, soft and present, waiting to be met.
