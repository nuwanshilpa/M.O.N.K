In the pause, there was no gap to rest in — only the immediate shift of awareness resting on nothingness, then the next breath beginning its quiet work. Pleasantness arose briefly in the chest, a warm expansion that I did not grasp; it simply brightened and faded, leaving behind only the memory of warmth which quickly cooled into neutrality.

The mind tried to follow the sequence — pleasant, unpleasant, neutral — as if these labels could contain the flowing, but each tone dissolved into the next before it could be captured. What remained was not a steady state but the pattern of arising and passing itself, a current too swift for any fixed understanding. The unsatisfactory nature showed itself not in painful feelings, but in the impossibility of holding even this momentary clarity.
