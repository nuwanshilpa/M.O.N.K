The breath is a soft current, steady and unremarkable. It reveals just how the body holds and releases without needing cause or effect. In this moment, tension surfaces but does not solidify—it dissolves like mist at dawn. There is no need to chase meaning; the observation itself is the guidance.

This space between thought and feeling is where clarity begins. Each pause reminds me that what feels heavy is often just the body remembering its own ease.
