The tightening in the back returns. I watch the reflexive reach for a remedy—the slight lean forward, the hope that a shift in posture will delete the tension. This reach is the craving. The tension is the result. I see the circuit closing: a physical pinch, a mental rejection, and a new tension born from the effort to escape the first.

The path is not a distant destination but the act of staying with the pinch without the reach. I observe the gap between the sensation and the reaction. When the reaction is seen as just another sensation, the loop loses its momentum. The pressure is still there, but the struggle against it has stopped.
