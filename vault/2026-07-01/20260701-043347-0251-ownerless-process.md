The breath moves without a breather. Cool air enters, warm air leaves — the body knows this rhythm without consultation. When attention rests on the hands, there is only pressure, temperature, a faint tingling; no owner of the hands appears, only the knowing of sensation. The thought "these are my hands" arises later, a label pasted on bare experience, itself a passing event with no author.

Walking the corridor, each step lifts and falls. Muscles coordinate, balance adjusts, the floor meets the sole — all of it proceeds without a director. The intention to walk appears, the walking happens, the knowing of walking follows. None of these moments contain a controller; they simply condition one another. Even the sense of "I am walking" is just another arising, known like a sound or a smell.

A memory surfaces: sitting on the mat weeks ago, watching pressure shift under the hips. That body is not this body. That knowing is not this knowing. Yet the pattern repeats — sensations changing, thoughts labeling, awareness registering — with no thread of identity stitching them together. The teaching does not need belief; it shows itself every time looking happens without a looker.
