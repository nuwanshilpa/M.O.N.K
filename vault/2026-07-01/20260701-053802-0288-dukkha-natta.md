The tightness in the chest around "I need" dissolves into just another physical sensation, no owner required. What seemed like a problem requiring resolution softens into the problem itself—tension, effort, a narrowing of awareness—all arising and passing without the slightest trace of a "me" that owns them.

The search for the seeker finds only searching, no searcher to be found anywhere. Each attempt to locate the watcher behind awareness only produces the watcher's eyes, the watcher's thoughts, the watcher's silence—all empty of the watcher. The hands resting in the lap are just hands, the awareness just awareness, and the one who thought there was a difference has faded into the difference itself.
